# Test data

`bidask-ohlc.csv` and `bidask-ohlc-miss.csv` are the reference series of Ardia,
Guidotti and Kroencke's EDGE estimator, from
https://github.com/eguidotti/bidask (`pseudocode/`), MIT License,
Copyright (c) 2024 Emanuele Guidotti. The authors' own tests pin EDGE on them
to 0.0101849034905478, −0.016889917516422 (the first 10 rows, signed) and
0.01013284969780197; `tests/liquidity.py` pins `gr.liquidity.edge` to the same.

`rmgarch_dcc_norm.json` and `dji30ret_5.csv` are rmgarch 1.4.2's `dccfit` on
the first 1,000 returns of five `dji30ret` stocks (AA, AXP, BA, BAC, C) × 100,
with sGARCH(1,1)-normal margins and a DCC(1,1)-mvnorm stage 2, as generated by
`scripts/make_fixtures.R` in https://github.com/marwinsteiner/pymgarch
(commit a7c7b68), MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Marwin Steiner. The JSON keeps
the `dcc_norm` entry only: R's a = 0.0108409458507285 and b = 0.786948216873906,
its joint log-likelihood −10111.2993183835, Q̄, the last R, the one-step R
forecast, and the residuals and σ. `tests/corr.py` evaluates
`gr.models.corr`'s filter and likelihood at R's parameters on R's residuals, and
re-estimates (a, b) end to end, the two levels pymgarch checks.
